About Afford-X

Afford-X is an Information & Communications Technology (ICT) company operating in the adtech space. Our mission is to align consumer advertising with real affordability, so marketers reach audiences who are not just interested, but genuinely able to purchase.

The Affordability Language Model (ALM)

At the core of Afford-X is the Affordability Language Model (ALM), a proprietary AI agent trained on nearly one billion data points. ALM matches consumer intent with financial reality, giving marketers a first-in-market exclusion filter that removes audiences unlikely to convert.

Exclusion-First Targeting

Filters out non-converting and non-affording audiences from the start, optimizing spend before a campaign even launches.

Real-Time Adaptability

ALM continuously adapts to shifts in audience behavior and purchasing trends to keep targeting efficient.

Advanced Segmentation

Combines demographic, intent, and affordability signals for precise, purchase-propensity-driven audience scoring.

Plug-and-Play SaaS

A full-stack platform — database, authentication, and billing ready — so teams can integrate and deploy quickly.
